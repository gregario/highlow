# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 2026*

## The short version

High Low does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking.

## What we collect

Nothing.

The game runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no database, and no API. Your gameplay data (including your personal best score) is stored in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

## Local storage

The game uses your browser's local storage to save one piece of data: your highest streak score. This is a single number stored on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, not accessible to the game's creator, and not shared with any third party.

If you clear your browser data or use private browsing mode, this number is not saved. The game works identically either way.

## Third-party services

The game loads a web font (Inter) from Google Fonts. When your browser requests this font, Google's servers receive your IP address and standard HTTP request headers as part of the font delivery. This is the same as any website that uses web fonts. Google's privacy policy governs their handling of this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

No other third-party services are used. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising networks, no social media widgets, and no tracking pixels.

## Cookies

The game does not set any cookies.

## Children

The game does not collect any data from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no chat features, and no way for users to interact with each other.

## Your rights

Since we don't collect your data, there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or port. If you want to remove your locally stored best score, clear your browser's local storage for this site.

## Contact

This is a personal portfolio project. If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the creator through the contact information on the associated GitHub profile.
